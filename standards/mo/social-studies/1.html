📌 1. Knowledge of the principles expressed in documents shaping constitutional democracy in the United States 5 standards
MO.SS.1.B
Purposes and principles of the Constitution
Identify and explain why cities make laws.
MO.SS.1.C
Purposes and principles of the Bill of Rights
Discuss how individual rights are protected.
MO.SS.1.D
Role of citizens and governments in carrying out constitutional principles
Give examples of being an active and informed citizen in your classroom or community.
MO.SS.1.E
Character traits and civic attitudes of significant individuals
Describe the character traits of role models within your community.
MO.SS.1.F
Knowledge of the symbols of our state and nation
Recognize and explain the significance of the Statue of Liberty, U.S. Capitol, Bald Eagle and the Liberty Bell.
📌 2. Knowledge of principles and processes of governance systems 2 standards
MO.SS.2.C
Processes of governmental systems in decision making
Describe how authoritative decisions are made, enforced and interpreted within schools and local communities.
MO.SS.2.D
Functions of governmental systems
Describe roles and responsibilities of people in government, such as a judge, mayor, police, city council member, in a community.

MO.SS.3a.A
Understand the movement of people from many regions of the world to North America

MO.SS.3a.B
Historical perspective / Thinking / Passage of time

MO.SS.3a.C
Knowledge of the contributions of significant persons in U.S. history.
