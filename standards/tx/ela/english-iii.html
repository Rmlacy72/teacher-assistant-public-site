📌 Composition: writing process 5 standards
E3.9.A
Plan writing by generating ideas
Plan a piece of writing appropriate for various purposes and audiences by generating ideas through a range of strategies such as brainstorming, journaling, reading, or discussing.
E3.9.B
Develop focused drafts using rhetorical devices
Develop drafts into a focused, structured, and coherent piece of writing in timed and open-ended situations by: (i) using strategic organizational structures appropriate to purpose, audience, topic, and context; and (ii) developing an engaging idea reflecting depth of thought with effective use of rhetorical devices, details, examples, and commentary.
E3.9.C
Revise for sentence fluency
Revise drafts to improve clarity, development, organization, style, diction, and sentence fluency, both within and between sentences.
E3.9.D
Edit drafts using a style guide
Edit drafts to demonstrate a command of standard English conventions using a style guide as appropriate.
E3.9.E
Publish for appropriate audiences
Publish written work for appropriate audiences.

📌 Developing and sustaining foundational language skills: oral language 4 standards
E3.1.A
Evaluate coherence and critique speaker choices
Engage in meaningful and respectful discourse when evaluating the clarity and coherence of a speaker's message and critiquing the impact of a speaker's use of diction and syntax.
E3.1.B
Follow complex instructions and respond appropriately
Follow and give complex instructions, clarify meaning by asking pertinent questions, and respond appropriately.
E3.1.C
Give a well-structured formal presentation
Give a formal presentation that exhibits a logical structure, smooth transitions, accurate evidence, well-chosen details, and rhetorical devices and that employs eye contact, speaking rate such as pauses for effect, volume, enunciation, purposeful gestures, and conventions of language to communicate ideas effectively.
E3.1.D
Participate collaboratively evaluating group work
Participate collaboratively, offering ideas or judgments that are purposeful in moving the team toward goals, asking relevant and insightful questions, tolerating a range of positions and ambiguity in decision making, and evaluating the work of the group based on agreed-upon criteria.

📌 Multiple genres: genres 6 standards
E3.7.A
Read and analyze American literature
Read and analyze American literature across literary periods.
E3.7.B
Analyze poetry characteristics and sound devices
Analyze relationships among characteristics of poetry, including stanzas, line breaks, speaker, and sound devices in poems across a variety of poetic forms.
E3.7.C
Analyze dramatic elements advancing the plot
Analyze how the relationships among dramatic elements advance the plot.
E3.7.D
Analyze informational text characteristics
Analyze characteristics and structural elements of informational texts such as: (i) clear thesis, strong supporting evidence, pertinent examples, commentary, summary, and conclusion; and (ii) the relationship between organizational design and author's purpose.
E3.7.E
Analyze argumentative text characteristics
Analyze characteristics and structural elements of argumentative texts such as: (i) clear arguable thesis, appeals, structure of the argument, convincing conclusion, and call to action; (ii) various types of evidence and treatment of counterarguments, including concessions and rebuttals; and (iii) identifiable audience or reader.
E3.7.F
Analyze effectiveness of multimodal/digital texts
Analyze the effectiveness of characteristics of multimodal and digital texts.
