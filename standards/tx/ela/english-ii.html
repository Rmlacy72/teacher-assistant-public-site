📌 Composition: writing process 5 standards
E2.9.A
Plan writing by generating ideas
Plan a piece of writing appropriate for various purposes and audiences by generating ideas through a range of strategies such as brainstorming, journaling, reading, or discussing.
E2.9.B
Develop focused drafts with commentary
Develop drafts into a focused, structured, and coherent piece of writing in timed and open-ended situations by: (i) using an organizing structure appropriate to purpose, audience, topic, and context; and (ii) developing an engaging idea reflecting depth of thought with specific details, examples, and commentary.
E2.9.C
Revise for clarity and sentence effectiveness
Revise drafts to improve clarity, development, organization, style, diction, and sentence effectiveness, including use of parallel constructions and placement of phrases and dependent clauses.
E2.9.D
Edit drafts using conventions
Edit drafts using standard English conventions, including: (i) a variety of complete, controlled sentences and avoidance of unintentional splices, run-ons, and fragments; (ii) consistent, appropriate use of verb tense and active and passive voice; (iii) pronoun-antecedent agreement; (iv) correct capitalization; (v) punctuation, including commas, semicolons, colons, dashes, and parentheses to set off phrases and clauses as appropriate; and (vi) correct spelling.
E2.9.E
Publish for appropriate audiences
Publish written work for appropriate audiences.

📌 Developing and sustaining foundational language skills: oral language 4 standards
E2.1.A
Engage in respectful discourse
Engage in meaningful and respectful discourse by listening actively, responding appropriately, and adjusting communication to audiences and purposes.
E2.1.B
Follow complex oral instructions
Follow and give complex oral instructions to perform specific tasks, answer questions, or solve problems and complex processes.
E2.1.C
Give a formal thesis-driven presentation
Give a formal presentation that incorporates a clear thesis and a logical progression of valid evidence from reliable sources and that employs eye contact, speaking rate such as pauses for effect, volume, enunciation, purposeful gestures, and conventions of language to communicate ideas effectively.
E2.1.D
Participate collaboratively toward consensus
Participate collaboratively, building on the ideas of others, contributing relevant information, developing a plan for consensus building, and setting ground rules for decision making.

📌 Multiple genres: genres 6 standards
E2.7.A
Read and analyze world literature
Read and analyze world literature across literary periods.
E2.7.B
Analyze metrics, rhyme schemes, conventions in poems
Analyze the effects of metrics; rhyme schemes; types of rhymes such as end, internal, slant, and eye; and other conventions in poems across a variety of poetic forms.
E2.7.C
Analyze dramatic conventions
Analyze the function of dramatic conventions such as asides, soliloquies, dramatic irony, and satire.
E2.7.D
Analyze informational text characteristics
Analyze characteristics and structural elements of informational texts such as: (i) clear thesis, relevant supporting evidence, pertinent examples, and conclusion; and (ii) the relationship between organizational design and thesis.
E2.7.E
Analyze argumentative text characteristics
Analyze characteristics and structural elements of argumentative texts such as: (i) clear arguable claim, appeals, and convincing conclusion; (ii) various types of evidence and treatment of counterarguments, including concessions and rebuttals; and (iii) identifiable audience or reader.
E2.7.F
Analyze multimodal/digital texts
Analyze characteristics of multimodal and digital texts.
