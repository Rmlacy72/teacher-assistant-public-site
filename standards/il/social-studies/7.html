📌 Civics 14 standards
SS.6-8.CV.1.LC
Civic and Political Institutions
Identify different types of citizenship and immigration statuses of individuals in the United States and the different rights and roles (e.g., voters, jurors, taxpayers, military, protesters, and officeholders) based on citizenship or immigration status. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.CV.2.LC
Civic and Political Institutions
Describe the origins and purposes and impact of governing documents and policies (e.g., U.S. Constitution, Illinois Constitution, laws, treaties, and international agreements) and synthesize their application and impacts on multiple groups of people with how they have changed over time. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.CV.1.MdC
Civic and Political Institutions
Describe and analyze the roles of political, civil, and economic organizations from a national to local scale in impacting multiple individuals and communities. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.CV.2.MdC
Civic and Political Institutions
Explain the origins, functions, and structure of government with reference to the U.S. Constitution, Illinois Constitution, and other systems of government and how they have impacted multiple groups of people. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.CV.3.MdC
Civic and Political Institutions
Analyze the origins, purposes, and processes connected to maintaining or changing powers and how individuals and societies protect, grant, or deny rights. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.CV.1.MC
Civic and Political Institutions
Evaluate the powers and responsibilities of individuals, political parties, interest groups, and the media; how these have changed over time; and the impacts on multiple communities. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.CV.2.MC
Civic and Political Institutions
Analyze how the application of laws and the protection, granting, or denial of individual and collective rights have impacted participation and powers of various groups of people. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.CV.3.MC
Civic and Political Institutions
Synthesize how the changes over time in the application of laws and granting or denial of rights have impacted governments, public officials, and bureaucracies at different levels in the United States, U.S. territories, and tribal nations within the United States. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.CV.3.LC
Participation & Deliberation: Applying Civic Virtues and Democratic Principles
Identify the means used by individuals and groups to either maintain or change powers and protect, grant, or deny rights of individuals and communities in societies. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.CV.4.LC
Participation & Deliberation: Applying Civic Virtues and Democratic Principles
Analyze the relationships among democratic principles, civic virtues, special interests, and perspectives to the ideas and principles contained in the constitutions, laws, treaties, and international agreements of the United States and other countries. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.CV.4.MdC
Participation & Deliberation: Applying Civic Virtues and Democratic Principles
Analyze the origins, purposes, and processes connected to maintaining or changing powers and how individuals and societies protect, grant, or deny rights. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.CV.5.MdC
Participation & Deliberation: Applying Civic Virtues and Democratic Principles
Analyze and synthesize ideas and principles contained in the founding and governing documents and practices of the United States, U.S. territories, and tribal nations within the United States. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.CV.4.MC
Participation & Deliberation: Applying Civic Virtues and Democratic Principles
Explain how granting rights for individuals or communities does not reduce the ability of other individuals or communities to exercise the same rights and evaluate how efforts to maintain systems or initiate change within societies have worked for or against the benefit or oppression of multiple groups. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.CV.5.MC
Participation & Deliberation: Applying Civic Virtues and Democratic Principles
Evaluate and critique deliberative processes regarding the development and application of governing documents and policies and how they have changed over time. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
📌 Economics and Financial Literacy 15 standards

Economic Decision Making

Economic Decision Making
Explain how external benefits and costs influence choices. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.EC.1.MC
Economic Decision Making
Evaluate alternative approaches or solutions to current economic issues in terms of benefits and costs for different groups and society as a whole. This standard applies to grades 6-8.

Exchange and Markets

SS.6-8.EC.2.MdC

SS.6-8.EC.2.MC
Exchange and Markets

National and Global Economy

SS.6-8.EC.3.MdC
National and Global Economy

SS.6-8.EC.3.MC
National and Global Economy
Evaluate employment, unemployment, inflation, total production, income and economic growth data and how they affect different groups. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.EC.FL.1.LC

SS.6-8.EC.FL.2.LC
Financial Literacy
Explain the roles and relationships between savers, borrowers, interest, time, and the purposes for saving. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.EC.FL.1.MdC
Financial Literacy

SS.6-8.EC.FL.2.MdC
Financial Literacy

SS.6-8.EC.FL.1.MC
Financial Literacy
Describe the connection between credit, credit options, and interest and credit history. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.EC.FL.2.MC
Financial Literacy
Analyze the relationship between financial risks and protection, insurance and costs. This standard applies to grades 6-8.

SS.6-8.G.1.MdC
Geographic Representations

SS.6-8.G.1.MC
Geographic Representations

Human-Environment Interaction

SS.6-8.G.2.MdC

SS.6-8.G.2.MC
Human-Environment Interaction

SS.6-8.G.3.MdC
Human Population

SS.6-8.G.3.MC
Human Population
Evaluate the influences of long-term, human-induced environmental change on spatial patterns of conflict and cooperation. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.G.4.LC
Global Interconnections
Identify how cultural and environmental characteristics vary among regions of the world. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.G.4.MdC

SS.6-8.G.4.MC

📌 History 12 standards
SS.6-8.H.1.LC
Change, Continuity, and Context
Identify and describe the contexts of a series of historical events and developments as examples of change and/or continuity based on the perspectives of multiple diverse groups. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.H.1.MdC
Change, Continuity, and Context
Analyze connections and disconnections among events and developments in broader historical contexts and identify the effects of events on groups of people who have been marginalized. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.H.1.MC
Change, Continuity, and Context
Evaluate the significance of historical events to multiple groups and the relationship to modern-day movements and events. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.H.2.LC
Perspectives
Explain how and why perspectives of people have changed over time. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.H.2.MdC
Perspectives
Analyze and compare multiple factors that influenced the perspectives of multiple groups of people during different historical eras relevant to the cultural groups. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.H.2.MC
Perspectives
Evaluate how perspectives of multiple individuals and groups have impacted the availability of information and the creation of historical sources. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.H.3.LC
Historical Sources and Evidence
Classify the kinds of historical sources used in a secondary interpretation to include sources representing multiple perspectives. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.H.3.MdC
Historical Sources and Evidence
Detect possible biases and limitations in the historical record based on evidence collected from different kinds of historical sources by identifying the author, purpose, funding/source, type of publication/outlet, intended audience, etc. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.H.3.MC
Historical Sources and Evidence
Evaluate the validity of claims made in historical sources representing a variety of perspectives, describe the possible or actual impact(s) of the sources on audiences, and determine uses and applications. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.H.4.LC
Causation and Argumentation
Describe the differences between correlation and causation in historical events and explain multiple causes and effects of historical events. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.H.4.MdC
Causation and Argumentation
Compare and contrast the central historical arguments in secondary works across multiple media. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.H.4.MC
Causation and Argumentation
Organize and critique applicable evidence to develop a coherent argument about the past. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
📌 Inquiry Skills 16 standards

SS.6-8.IS.4.MC
Evaluating Sources and Using Evidence
Gather relevant information from credible sources and determine whether they support each other. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.IS.5.LC
Evaluating Sources and Using Evidence

SS.6-8.IS.5.MdC
Evaluating Sources and Using Evidence
Identify evidence from multiple sources to support claims, noting any limitations of the evidence. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.IS.5.MC
Evaluating Sources and Using Evidence
Develop claims and counterclaims using evidence from credible sources while pointing out the strengths and limitations of both. This standard applies to grades 6-8.

SS.6-8.IS.6.MdC

SS.6-8.IS.6.MC
Communicating Conclusions and Taking Informed Action

SS.6-8.IS.7.LC
Communicating Conclusions and Taking Informed Action

SS.6-8.IS.8.LC
Communicating Conclusions and Taking Informed Action
Analyze how a problem can manifest itself, identify the individuals and communities impacted by a problem, and address the challenges and opportunities faced by those trying to take action toward solutions. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.IS.8.MdC
Communicating Conclusions and Taking Informed Action
Assess individual and collective capacities within a given context to take action, address problems, and identify potential outcomes. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
SS.6-8.IS.8.MC
Communicating Conclusions and Taking Informed Action
Apply a range of deliberative and democratic procedures to make decisions and take action in schools and community contexts. This standard applies to grades 6-8.
