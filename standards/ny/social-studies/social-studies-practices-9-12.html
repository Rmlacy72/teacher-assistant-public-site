📌 Chronological Reasoning and Causation 8 standards
9-12.SSP.B1
Explain how events influence subsequent events
Articulate how events are related chronologically to one another in time and explain the ways in which earlier ideas and events may influence subsequent ideas and events.
9-12.SSP.B2
Identify causes and effects across periods
Identify causes and effects using examples from different time periods and courses of study across several grade levels.
9-12.SSP.B3
Evaluate relationships between causes and effects
Identify, analyze, and evaluate the relationship between multiple causes and effects.
9-12.SSP.B4
Distinguish long-term vs. immediate causes
Distinguish between long-term and immediate causes and multiple effects (time, continuity, and change).
9-12.SSP.B5
Evaluate dynamics of historical continuity
Recognize, analyze, and evaluate dynamics of historical continuity and change over periods of time and investigate factors that caused those changes over time.
9-12.SSP.B6
Recognize periodization favors certain narratives
Recognize that choice of specific periodizations favors or advantages one narrative, region, or group over another narrative, region, or group.
9-12.SSP.B7
Relate continuity/change to historical processes
Relate patterns of continuity and change to larger historical processes and themes.
9-12.SSP.B8
Construct models of historical periodization
Describe, analyze, evaluate, and construct models of historical periodization that historians use to categorize events.
📌 Civic Participation 8 standards
9-12.SSP.F1
Provide evidence for counter-arguments respectfully
Demonstrate respect for the rights of others in discussions and classroom debates; respectfully disagree with other viewpoints and provide evidence for a counter-argument.
9-12.SSP.F2
Participate in civic issues
Participate in activities that focus on a classroom, school, community, state, or national issue or problem.
9-12.SSP.F3
Explain philosophies of political participation
Explain differing philosophies of social and political participation and the role of the individual leading to group-driven philosophies.
9-12.SSP.F4
Contrast roles of the individual across societies
Identify, describe, and contrast the roles of the individual in opportunities for social and political participation in different societies.
9-12.SSP.F5
Participate in resolving conflicts
Participate in persuading, debating, negotiating, and compromising in the resolution of conflicts and differences.
9-12.SSP.F6
Determine course of action for social issues
Identify situations in which social actions are required and determine an appropriate course of action.
9-12.SSP.F7
Influence power toward freedom and rights
Work to influence those in positions of power to strive for extensions of freedom, social justice, and human rights.
9-12.SSP.F8
Engage in the political process as a citizen
Fulfill social and political responsibilities associated with citizenship in a democratic society and interdependent global community by developing awareness of and/or engaging in the political process.
📌 Comparison and Contextualization 6 standards
9-12.SSP.C1
Relate geography to historical outcomes
Identify similarities and differences between geographic regions across historical time periods, and relate differences in geography to different historical events and outcomes.
9-12.SSP.C2
Evaluate multiple historical perspectives
Identify, compare, and evaluate multiple perspectives on a given historical experience.
9-12.SSP.C3
Compare developments across contexts
Identify and compare similarities and differences between historical developments over time and in different geographical and cultural contexts.
9-12.SSP.C4
Evaluate multiple historical developments
Describe, compare, and evaluate multiple historical developments (within societies; across and between societies; in various chronological and geographical contexts).
9-12.SSP.C5
Relate geography, economics, and history
Recognize the relationship between geography, economics, and history as a context for events and movements and as a matrix of time and place.
9-12.SSP.C6
Connect developments to broader processes
Connect historical developments to specific circumstances of time and place and to broader regional, national, or global processes and draw connections to the present (where appropriate).
📌 Economics and Economic Systems 6 standards
9-12.SSP.E1
Use marginal benefits/costs in arguments
Use marginal benefits and marginal costs to construct an argument for or against an approach or solution to an economic issue.
9-12.SSP.E2
Analyze how incentives influence markets
Analyze the ways in which incentives influence what is produced and distributed in a market system.
9-12.SSP.E3
Evaluate competition in specific markets
Evaluate the extent to which competition between sellers and between buyers exists in specific markets.
9-12.SSP.E4
Describe property rights and rule of law
Describe concepts of property rights and rule of law as they apply to a market economy.
9-12.SSP.E5
Use economic indicators to analyze the economy
Use economic indicators to analyze the current and future state of the economy.
9-12.SSP.E6
Analyze government economic policy effects
Analyze government economic policies and the effects on the national and global economy.
📌 Gathering, Interpreting, and Using Evidence 7 standards
9-12.SSP.A1
Form and answer historical hypotheses
Define and frame questions about events and the world in which we live, form hypotheses as potential answers to these questions, use evidence to answer these questions, and consider and analyze counter-hypotheses.
9-12.SSP.A2
Evaluate evidence from diverse sources
Identify, describe, and evaluate evidence about events from diverse sources (including written documents, works of art, photographs, charts and graphs, artifacts, oral traditions, and other primary and secondary sources).
9-12.SSP.A3
Analyze evidence for bias and audience
Analyze evidence in terms of content, authorship, point of view, bias, purpose, format, and audience.
9-12.SSP.A4
Evaluate arguments of others
Describe, analyze, and evaluate arguments of others.
9-12.SSP.A5
Draw conclusions from evidence
Make inferences and draw conclusions from evidence.
9-12.SSP.A6
Construct persuasive arguments using evidence
Deconstruct and construct plausible and persuasive arguments, using evidence.
9-12.SSP.A7
Fuse evidence into understandings of the past
Create meaningful and persuasive understandings of the past by fusing disparate and relevant evidence from primary and secondary sources and drawing connections to the present.
📌 Geographic Reasoning 6 standards
9-12.SSP.D1
Ask geographic questions about locations
Ask geographic questions about where places are located, why their locations are important, and how their locations are related to the locations of other places and people.
9-12.SSP.D2
Use geographic tools for spatial context
Identify, describe, and evaluate the relationships between people, places, regions, and environments by using geographic tools to place them in a spatial context.
9-12.SSP.D3
Evaluate environment-human activity relationships
Identify, analyze, and evaluate the relationship between the environment and human activities, how the physical environment is modified by human activities, and how human activities are also influenced by Earth’s physical features and processes.
9-12.SSP.D4
Interpret relationships between patterns/processes
Recognize and interpret (at different scales) the relationships between patterns and processes.
9-12.SSP.D5
Analyze how place/region influence civilizations
Recognize and analyze how place and region influence the social, cultural, and economic characteristics of civilizations.
9-12.SSP.D6
Analyze changing connections between regions
Characterize and analyze changing connections between places and regions.
