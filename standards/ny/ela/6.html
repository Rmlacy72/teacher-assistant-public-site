📌 Language Standards — Vocabulary Acquisition and Use 10 standards
6L4 Determine meaning choosing flexibly from strategies 4 sub-parts
Determine or clarify the meaning of unknown and multiple-meaning words and phrases, choosing flexibly from a range of strategies.
6L4a
Use context as clue to meaning
Use context (e.g., the overall meaning of a sentence or paragraph; a word's position or function in a sentence) as a clue to the meaning of a word or phrase.
6L4b
Use Greek/Latin affixes and roots as clues
Use common, grade-appropriate Greek or Latin affixes and roots as clues to the meaning of a word (e.g., audience, auditory, audible).
6L4c
Consult reference materials for pronunciation/meaning
Consult reference materials (e.g., dictionaries, glossaries, thesauruses) to find the pronunciation of a word or determine or clarify its precise meaning or its part of speech.
6L4d
Verify preliminary determination of meaning
Verify the preliminary determination of the meaning of a word or phrase (e.g., by checking the inferred meaning in context or in a dictionary).
6L5 Demonstrate understanding of figurative language 3 sub-parts
Demonstrate understanding of figurative language, word relationships, and nuances in word meanings.
6L5a
Interpret figurative language including personification
Interpret figurative language, including personification, in context.
6L5b
Use word relationships to understand words
Use the relationship between particular words (e.g., cause/effect, part/whole, item/category) to better understand each of the words.
6L5c
Distinguish connotations of similar-denotation words
Distinguish among the connotations of words with similar denotations (e.g., stingy, scrimping, economical, unwasteful, thrifty).
6L6
Acquire/use academic and content-specific words
Acquire and accurately use general academic and content-specific words and phrases; apply vocabulary knowledge when considering a word or phrase important to comprehension or expression.
📌 Reading Standards (Literary and Informational Text) — Craft and Structure 3 standards
6R4
Determine meaning, analyze impact of word choices
Determine the meaning of words and phrases as they are used in a text, including figurative and connotative meanings. Analyze the impact of specific word choices on meaning, tone, and mood, including words with multiple meanings. (RI&RL)
6R5
Analyze how sections fit into overall structure
In literary texts, analyze how a particular sentence, paragraph, stanza, chapter, scene, or section fits into the overall structure of a text and how it contributes to the development of theme, central idea, setting, or plot. (RL) In informational texts, analyze how a particular sentence, paragraph, chapter, or section fits into the overall structure of a text and how it contributes to the development of theme or central ideas. (RI)
6R6
Identify point of view / explain author's perspective
In literary texts, identify the point of view and explain how it is developed and conveys meaning. (RL) In informational texts, explain how an author's geographic location or culture affects his or her perspective. (RI)
📌 Reading Standards (Literary and Informational Text) — Integration of Knowledge and Ideas 3 standards
6R7
Compare formats' contribution to understanding subject
Compare and contrast how different formats, including print and digital media, contribute to the understanding of a subject. (RI&RL)
6R8
Trace/evaluate argument, distinguish supported claims
Trace and evaluate the development of an argument and specific claims in texts, distinguishing claims that are supported by reasons and relevant evidence from claims that are not. (RI&RL)
6R9
Use criteria to evaluate quality, connect to other texts
Use established criteria in order to evaluate the quality of texts. Make connections to other texts, ideas, cultural perspectives, eras, and personal experiences. (RI&RL)
📌 Reading Standards (Literary and Informational Text) — Key Ideas and Details 3 standards
6R1
Cite evidence to support analysis, make inferences
Cite textual evidence to support an analysis of what the text says explicitly/implicitly and make logical inferences. (RI&RL)
6R2
Determine theme/central idea, summarize
Determine a theme or central idea of a text and how it is developed by key supporting details over the course of a text; summarize a text (RI&RL)
6R3
Describe how events unfold / analyze introduction of ideas
In literary texts, describe how events unfold, as well as how characters respond or change as the plot moves toward a resolution. (RL) In informational texts, analyze how individuals, events, and ideas are introduced, relate to each other, and are developed. (RI)
📌 Speaking and Listening Standards — Comprehension and Collaboration 7 standards
6SL1 Engage effectively in collaborative discussions 4 sub-parts
Engage effectively in a range of collaborative discussions with diverse partners; express ideas clearly and persuasively, and build on those of other.
6SL1a
Come prepared, refer to evidence to probe ideas
Come to discussions prepared, having read or studied required material; draw on that preparation by referring to evidence on the topic, text, or issue to probe and reflect on ideas under discussion.
6SL1b
Follow norms, set goals, define roles
Follow norms for collegial discussions, set specific goals and deadlines, and define individual roles as needed.
6SL1c
Pose/respond to questions with elaboration
Pose and respond to specific questions with elaboration and detail by making comments that contribute to the topic, text, or issue under discussion.
6SL1d
Demonstrate understanding through reflection/paraphrasing
Consider the ideas expressed and demonstrate understanding of multiple perspectives through reflection and paraphrasing.
6SL2
Interpret info presented in diverse formats
Interpret information presented in diverse formats (e.g., including visual, quantitative, and oral) and explain how it relates to a topic, text, or issue under study.
6SL3
Delineate speaker's argument, distinguish supported claims
Delineate a speaker's argument and specific claims, distinguishing claims that are supported by reasons and evidence from claims that are not.
📌 Speaking and Listening Standards — Presentation of Knowledge and Ideas 3 standards
6SL4
Present claims/findings sequencing ideas logically
Present claims and findings, sequencing ideas logically and using relevant descriptions, facts, and details to accentuate central ideas or themes; use appropriate eye contact, adequate volume, and clear enunciation.
6SL5
Include digital media to clarify/emphasize ideas
Include digital media and/or visual displays in presentations to clarify information and emphasize and enhance central ideas or themes.
6SL6
Adapt speech demonstrating command of formal English
Adapt speech to a variety of contexts and tasks, demonstrating command of formal English when indicated or appropriate.
📌 Writing Standards — Research to Build and Present Knowledge 2 standards
6W6
Conduct research, refocusing inquiry as needed
Conduct research to answer questions, including self-generated questions, drawing on multiple sources and refocusing the inquiry when appropriate.
6W7
Gather info, assess credibility, avoid plagiarism
Gather relevant information from multiple sources; assess the credibility of each source; quote or paraphrase the data and conclusions of others; avoid plagiarism and provide basic bibliographic information for sources.
📌 Writing Standards — Text Types and Purposes 22 standards
6W1 Write arguments with clear reasons/evidence 6 sub-parts
Write arguments to support claims with clear reasons and relevant evidence.
6W1a
Introduce claim, distinguish from counterclaim
Introduce a precise claim, acknowledge and distinguish the claim from a counterclaim, and organize the reasons and evidence logically.
6W1b
Support claims with credible sources
Support claim(s) with clear reasons and relevant evidence, using credible sources while demonstrating an understanding of the topic or text.
6W1c
Use precise language/content-specific vocab to argue
Use precise language and content-specific vocabulary to argue a claim.
6W1d
Use transitions to create cohesion
Use appropriate transitions to create cohesion and clarify the relationships among ideas and concepts.
6W1e
Provide concluding statement explaining significance
Provide a concluding statement or section that explains the significance of the argument presented.
6W1f
Maintain appropriate style and tone
Maintain a style and tone appropriate to the writing task.
6W2 Write informative text conveying ideas/concepts 6 sub-parts
Write informative/explanatory texts to examine a topic and convey ideas, concepts, and information through the selection, organization, and analysis of relevant content.
6W2a
Introduce topic, organize using strategies
Introduce a topic clearly; organize ideas, concepts, and information using strategies such as definition, classification, comparison/contrast, and cause/effect.
6W2b
Develop topic with facts/definitions/multimedia
Develop a topic with relevant facts, definitions, concrete details, quotations, or other information and examples; include formatting, graphics, and multimedia when useful to aid comprehension.
6W2c
Use precise language/content-specific vocab to explain
Use precise language and content-specific vocabulary to explain a topic.
6W2d
Use transitions to create cohesion
Use appropriate transitions to create cohesion and clarify the relationships among ideas and concepts.
6W2e
Provide concluding statement explaining significance
Provide a concluding statement or section that explains the significance of the information presented.
6W2f
Establish/maintain appropriate style
Establish and maintain a style appropriate to the writing task.
6W3 Write narratives with effective techniques/sequencing 5 sub-parts
Write narratives to develop real or imagined experiences or events using effective techniques, descriptive details and sequencing
6W3a
Engage reader by introducing narrator/characters
Engage the reader by introducing a narrator and/or characters.
6W3b
Use narrative techniques like dialogue/description
Use narrative techniques, such as dialogue and description, to develop experiences, events, and/or characters.
6W3c
Use transitional words to convey sequence
Use a variety of transitional words, phrases, and clauses to convey sequence and signal shifts from one time frame or setting to another.
6W3d
Use precise words/sensory language
Use precise words and phrases, relevant descriptive details, and sensory language to convey experiences and events.
6W3e
Provide conclusion reflecting on narrative
Provide a conclusion that follows from and reflects on what is experienced, observed, or resolved over the course of the narrative.
6W4
Create poem/story/play/artwork response
Create a poem, story, play, artwork, or other response to a text, author, theme, or personal experience.
6W5
Draw evidence from texts, apply reading standards
Draw evidence from literary or informational texts to support analysis, reflection, and research. Apply the grade 6 Reading Standards to both literary and informational text, where applicable.
