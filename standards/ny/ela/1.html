📌 Language Standards — Vocabulary Acquisition and Use 10 standards
1L4 Determine meaning choosing flexibly from strategies 3 sub-parts
Determine or clarify the meaning of unknown and multiple-meaning words and phrases, choosing flexibly from an array of strategies.
1L4a
Use sentence-level context as clue to meaning
Use sentence-level context as a clue to the meaning of a word or phrase.
1L4b
Use frequently occurring affixes as clue
Use frequently occurring affixes as a clue to the meaning of a word.
1L4c
Identify root words and inflectional forms
Identify frequently occurring root words (e.g., look) and their inflectional forms (e.g., looks, looked, looking).
1L5 Demonstrate understanding of word relationships 4 sub-parts
Demonstrate understanding of word relationships and nuances in word meanings.
1L5a
Sort words into categories
Sort words into categories (e.g., colors, clothing) to gain a sense of the concepts the categories represent.
1L5b
Define words by category and key attributes
Define words by category and by one or more key attributes (e.g., a duck is a bird that swims; a tiger is a large cat with stripes).
1L5c
Use words for identification/description
Use words for identification and description, making connections between words and their use (e.g., places at home that are cozy).
1L5d
Distinguish shades of meaning among verbs/adjectives
Distinguish shades of meaning among verbs differing in manner (e.g., look, peek, glance, stare, glare, scowl) and adjectives differing in intensity (e.g., large, gigantic) by defining or choosing them or by acting out the meanings.
1L6
Use words/phrases including conjunctions
Use words and phrases acquired through conversations, reading and being read to, and responding to texts, including using frequently occurring conjunctions to signal simple relationships (e.g., because).

📌 Reading Standards: Foundational Skills — Fluency 3 standards
1RF4 Read beginning texts with accuracy/fluency 2 sub-parts
Read beginning reader texts, appropriate to individual student ability, with sufficient accuracy and fluency to support comprehension.
1RF4a
Read texts orally with accuracy/rate/expression
Read beginning reader texts, appropriate to individual student ability, orally with accuracy, appropriate rate, and expression on successive readings.
1RF4b
Use context to self-correct word recognition
Use context to confirm or self-correct word recognition and understanding, rereading as necessary.
📌 Reading Standards: Foundational Skills — Phonics and Word Recognition 8 standards
1RF3 Apply phonics and word analysis skills 7 sub-parts
Know and apply phonics and word analysis skills in decoding words.
1RF3a
Know letter-sound correspondences for blends/digraphs
Know the letter-sound correspondences for common blends and consonant digraphs (e.g. sh, ch, th).
1RF3b
Decode long vowel sounds in one-syllable words
Decode long vowel sounds in regularly spelled one-syllable words (e.g., final –e conventions and common vowel teams).
1RF3c
Decode regularly spelled one-syllable words
Decode regularly spelled one-syllable words.
1RF3d
Determine number of syllables in a word
Determine the number of syllables in a printed word by using knowledge that every syllable must have a vowel sound.
1RF3e
Decode two-syllable words by breaking into syllables
Decode two-syllable words following basic patterns by breaking the words into syllables.
1RF3f
Recognize root words and simple suffixes
Recognize and identify root words and simple suffixes (e.g. run, runs, walk, walked)
1RF3g
Read common high-frequency words by sight
Read most common high-frequency words by sight
📌 Reading Standards: Foundational Skills — Phonological Awareness 4 standards

1RF2a

Count, blend and segment single syllable words that include consonant blends.
1RF2b

Create new words by manipulating individual sounds (phonemes) in spoken one-syllable words.
1RF2c
Manipulate phonemes in single-syllable words
Manipulate individual sounds (phonemes) in single-syllable spoken words.

📌 Speaking and Listening Standards — Comprehension and Collaboration 7 standards
1SL1 Participate in collaborative conversations 4 sub-parts
Participate in collaborative conversations with diverse peers and adults (e.g., in small and large groups and during play).
1SL1a

Follow agreed-upon rules for discussions and participate by actively listening, taking turns, and staying on topic.
1SL1b
Build on others' talk through exchanges
Build on others' talk in conversations by responding to the comments of others through multiple exchanges.
1SL1c
Ask questions to clear up confusion
Ask questions to clear up any confusion about topics and texts under discussion.
1SL1d
Consider individual differences communicating
Consider individual differences when communicating with others.
1SL2
Develop/answer questions about key details
Develop and answer questions about key details in diverse texts and formats.
1SL3
Develop/answer questions, identify speaker's view
Develop and answer questions to clarify what the speaker says and identify a speaker's point of view.
