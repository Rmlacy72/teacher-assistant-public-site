📌 Reading Standards for Literacy in Science and Technical Subjects (RST) 10 standards
RST.11-12.1
Cite evidence attending to gaps/inconsistencies
Cite specific textual evidence to support analysis of science and technical texts, attending to important distinctions the author makes and to any gaps or inconsistencies in the account.
RST.11-12.2
Summarize complex concepts by paraphrasing
Determine the central ideas or conclusions of a text; summarize complex concepts, processes, or information presented in a text by paraphrasing them in simpler but still accurate terms.
RST.11-12.3
Follow a complex procedure, analyze results
Follow precisely a complex multistep procedure when carrying out experiments, taking measurements, or performing technical tasks; analyze the specific results based on explanations in the text.
RST.11-12.4
Determine meaning of domain-specific symbols/terms
Determine the meaning of symbols, key terms, and other domain-specific words and phrases as they are used in a specific scientific or technical context relevant to grades 11-12 texts and topics.
RST.11-12.5
Analyze how text structures info into categories
Analyze how the text structures information or ideas into categories or hierarchies, demonstrating understanding of the information or ideas.
RST.11-12.6
Identify unresolved issues in author's purpose
Analyze the author's purpose in providing an explanation, describing a procedure, or discussing an experiment in a text, identifying important issues that remain unresolved.
RST.11-12.7
Integrate/evaluate multiple sources to solve a problem
Integrate and evaluate multiple sources of information presented in diverse formats and media (e.g., quantitative data, video, multimedia) in order to address a question or solve a problem.
RST.11-12.8
Evaluate hypotheses/data/conclusions, verify data
Evaluate the hypotheses, data, analysis, and conclusions in a science or technical text, verifying the data when possible and corroborating or challenging conclusions with other sources of information.
RST.11-12.9
Synthesize information into coherent understanding
Synthesize information from a range of sources (e.g., texts, experiments, simulations) into a coherent understanding of a process, phenomenon, or concept, resolving conflicting information when possible.
RST.11-12.10
Read and comprehend science/technical texts
By the end of grade 12, read and comprehend science/technical texts in the grades 11-12 text complexity band independently and proficiently.
