📌 Reading Standards for Literacy in Science and Technical Subjects (RST) 10 standards
RST.6-8.1
Cite textual evidence from science/technical texts
Cite specific textual evidence to support analysis of science and technical texts.
RST.6-8.2
Determine central ideas, provide accurate summary
Determine the central ideas or conclusions of a text; provide an accurate summary of the text distinct from prior knowledge or opinions.
RST.6-8.3
Follow a multistep procedure precisely
Follow precisely a multistep procedure when carrying out experiments, taking measurements, or performing technical tasks.
RST.6-8.4
Determine meaning of domain-specific symbols/terms
Determine the meaning of symbols, key terms, and other domain-specific words and phrases as they are used in a specific scientific or technical context relevant to grades 6-8 texts and topics.
RST.6-8.5
Analyze structure author uses to organize text
Analyze the structure an author uses to organize a text, including how the major sections contribute to the whole and to an understanding of the topic.
RST.6-8.6
Analyze author's purpose in a text
Analyze the author's purpose in providing an explanation, describing a procedure, or discussing an experiment in a text.
RST.6-8.7
Integrate quantitative info with a visual version
Integrate quantitative or technical information expressed in words in a text with a version of that information expressed visually (e.g., in a flowchart, diagram, model, graph, or table).
RST.6-8.8
Distinguish facts, judgment, speculation
Distinguish among facts, reasoned judgment based on research findings, and speculation in a text.
RST.6-8.9
Compare info from experiments/media to a text
Compare and contrast the information gained from experiments, simulations, video, or multimedia sources with that gained from reading a text on the same topic.
RST.6-8.10
Read and comprehend science/technical texts
By the end of grade 8, read and comprehend science/technical texts in the grades 6-8 text complexity band independently and proficiently.
