Student Data Privacy

Subprocessors

The third parties that process data on behalf of TeacherAssist.ai, what each one receives, and where it is processed.

Last updated: November 2026 Operator: TeacherAssist.ai, operated by LTC Inc., Illinois Contact: support@teacherassist.ai

This list supports schools' obligations under FERPA and the Illinois Student Online Personal Protection Act (SOPPA), including the requirement to publish the third parties that receive covered information. Every subprocessor that handles Student Data is bound by a written data processing agreement, and the AI providers are contractually prohibited from using it to train their models.

AI data handling. Data caching is disabled for Vertex AI. Under Microsoft's standard terms, Azure OpenAI may keep prompts and outputs for up to 30 days solely for abuse monitoring, reviewed only by authorized Microsoft personnel. Student work itself may contain a name or other information a student wrote, which is processed as part of the work.

Changes to this list

We notify contracted schools and institutions at least 30 days before adding or replacing a subprocessor that will process Student Data. A school may object within 15 days under its Data Processing Agreement. We also provide each contracted school an updated list of these third parties at least at the start of each state fiscal year and each calendar year.

Questions about this list can be sent to support@teacherassist.ai.
