Security & Privacy

Your students' data is safe.
Full stop.

TeacherAssist.ai was built by a team with 30+ years of enterprise healthcare technology experience — where data security isn't optional. We apply those same standards to every classroom.

🔐
Encrypted in Transit & at Rest

All data is encrypted using TLS 1.2+ in transit and AES-256 at rest. Student work is never transmitted or stored in plain text — from the moment you upload to the moment results are delivered.

🗑️
Uploaded Files Are Deleted After Use

Student PDFs are processed and immediately deleted from our servers once grading is complete. We do not retain student work, handwriting, or raw submissions beyond the active grading session.

🏛️
FERPA-Conscious Design

TeacherAssist.ai is designed with FERPA principles in mind. Student records stay under your control. We never sell, share, or use student data to train AI models. District data processing agreements available on request.

🔑
Secure Authentication

Access is protected by industry-standard Auth0 authentication — the same platform trusted by thousands of enterprise organizations. Every session is individually authenticated and time-limited.

☁️
Enterprise Cloud Infrastructure

Hosted on Microsoft Azure — the same cloud platform used by hospitals, financial institutions, and government agencies. Built-in redundancy, uptime monitoring, and automated backups protect your data around the clock.

👤
No Student PII Required

You control what identifying information is entered. Student names are optional — you can grade by ID or initials only. We never collect student email addresses, birthdays, or any personally identifiable information directly.
