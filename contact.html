Our Company

LTC Inc. — Technology that works in the real world

TeacherAssist.ai is a product of LTC Inc., an Illinois S-Corporation with a focused mission: build practical, high-quality technology solutions across industries.

LTC Inc. was founded on the principle that enterprise-grade thinking shouldn't be reserved for large organizations. The same discipline that drives technology at large healthcare systems — security, reliability, data integrity, and user trust — can be applied to solve real problems for teachers, small businesses, and specialized industries alike.

Headquartered in Illinois, LTC Inc. operates with the accountability of a local business and the technical depth of an enterprise consultancy.

Ron Lacy
Founder & Principal, LTC Inc.

Ron Lacy brings more than 30 years of technology leadership in large healthcare organizations — experience that shaped his understanding of what it takes to build systems that are secure, reliable, and trusted by professionals under real-world pressure.

After decades building and managing technology infrastructure for healthcare, Ron recognized that the same design principles — rigorous data handling, user-centered workflows, and meaningful analytics — could transform productivity in education. TeacherAssist.ai is the result of applying that enterprise discipline to the everyday challenges teachers face.
