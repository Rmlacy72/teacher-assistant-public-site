Welcome to TeacherAssist.ai

TeacherAssist.ai is an AI-powered platform built specifically for K–12 educators. Our tools help you save time, personalize instruction, and create standards-aligned materials — so you can focus on what matters most: your students.

This policy explains how the platform works, what we do with your data, and what you can expect from us as your technology partner. We've written it in plain language because you deserve to know exactly what you're agreeing to.

01

How We Use Artificial Intelligence

TeacherAssist.ai is powered by AI. Here's what that means for you.

✓ What our AI does
  • Grade both hand written and typed content
  • Provide feedback on student work aligned to your state standards
  • Generate curriculum, lesson plans, and instructional materials
  • Create differentiated instruction aligned to your State Standards
  • Provide suggested feedback on student work
  • Grades with your answer keys and rubrics
✕ What our AI does NOT do
  • Make final decisions about student grades — you always have final say
  • Access student PII without your explicit action
  • Use your inputs to train shared AI models
  • Misrepresent or fabricate curriculum standards

AI Models We Use

Our platform uses large language models (LLMs) provided by Google (Gemini) and Microsoft Azure (OpenAI GPT-4o). These models process the content you submit to grade papers and generate instructional materials. By using the platform, you acknowledge that your inputs are processed by these third-party AI providers in accordance with their respective terms of service and privacy policies.

AI Accuracy & Your Judgment

AI-generated content is a starting point, not a finished product. Always review AI output before sharing it with students. Our platform surfaces relevant standards (such as CC.5.NBT.1 or CC.8.EE.6) alongside generated content to help you verify alignment — but you are the curriculum expert.

Important Notice

TeacherAssist.ai does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or fitness for purpose of AI-generated content. Educators retain full professional responsibility for all materials shared with students.

02

Your Privacy & Data

We take your privacy seriously. Here's a plain-language breakdown of what we collect and why.

What We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and school or district (used to create your account and for billing)
  • Usage data: features you use, assignments you create, and grading activity (used to improve your experience)
  • Content you create: lesson plans, assignments, curricula, and grading results stored in your account
  • Billing information: managed securely through Stripe — we never store credit card numbers on our servers

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Student names, ID numbers, or personally identifiable information (PII) about your students
  • Biometric data, precise location data, or device fingerprinting beyond standard browser logs
  • Data from third-party platforms unless you explicitly connect them

How We Protect Your Data

All data is stored securely in Microsoft Azure infrastructure located in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and encrypted at rest. Access to your account data is limited to you and authorized TeacherAssist.ai personnel for support purposes only.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you cancel your account, you can request to have your data deleted.

FERPA & Student Data

FERPA Notice

TeacherAssist.ai is designed as a teacher productivity tool. We do not maintain a direct relationship with students and do not store student-level academic records. It is your responsibility to ensure that any student work you upload complies with your school or district's FERPA policies and applicable state privacy laws.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share data with trusted service providers (Azure, Stripe, Auth0) solely to operate the platform. These providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and may not use your data for their own purposes.

03

Standards Alignment

Everything TeacherAssist.ai generates is designed to connect to the standards you're already teaching.

Supported Standards

Our platform currently supports state standards Math, Science, Social Studies and Language Arts. Additional standards may be added and/or requested. Here is an example:

  • Mathematical Practices (MP.1–MP.8) for grades K–12
  • Grade-level domain standards for Kindergarten through Grade 8 (OA, NBT, NF, MD, G, RP, NS, EE, SP, F)
  • High school standards in Number & Quantity, Algebra, Functions, Geometry, and Statistics & Probability (grades 9–12)

How Standards Are Applied

When you create content, you can specify a grade level, domain strand, and standard code. The AI uses this information to generate aligned passages, questions, answer keys, and curriculum materials. Standard codes are displayed throughout the platform so you can verify alignment at a glance.

Standards Accuracy Disclaimer

While we work to maintain accurate standard mappings, AI-generated alignment suggestions should always be reviewed by you. Standards interpretations can vary by district and instructional context. TeacherAssist.ai does not warrant that generated content will satisfy any particular district, state, or federal compliance requirements.

Subscriptions & Billing

TeacherAssist.ai offers tiered subscription plans. Here's what you need to know.

Subscription Plans

We offer Free, Basic, and Professional plans. Features available to you depend on your active subscription. A full feature summary is available in your account Settings page at any time.

Free Trial

New users receive a free trial period to explore platform features. No credit card is required to start a trial. You will be notified before your trial ends, and no charges will occur without your consent.

Billing Terms

  • Payments are processed securely by Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment processor
  • Subscription fees are billed monthly or annually depending on your selected plan
  • You can manage, upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time from your Settings page
  • Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period — you retain full access until then
  • We do not offer refunds for partial billing periods, except where required by applicable law
  • We reserve the right to change pricing with at least 30 days' advance notice to active subscribers

Acceptable Use

TeacherAssist.ai is built for educators. Please use it responsibly.

✓ Permitted Use

  • Grading and providing feedback on student work
